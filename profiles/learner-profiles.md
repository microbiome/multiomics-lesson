## Is this lesson for me?

These are some of the people we had in mind when we wrote this lesson.

**Aino** is a PhD student in microbiology who has analysed 16S data in R.
Aino's project has stool samples with 16S sequencing and metabolomics from
the same people. Aino can analyse each table alone, but does not know how
to combine them, or which of the many published methods to use.

**Einari** is a researcher in nutrition who reads papers that link diet, gut
bacteria and metabolites. Einari wants to understand what methods such as
MOFA and DIABLO actually do, to judge the results of others and to run a
first integration of their own.

**Lumi** is a bioinformatician who has analysed transcriptomics and
proteomics together with Bioconductor. Lumi has been asked to add microbiome
data to a multi-omics project, and needs to know how microbiome data fits
into a `MultiAssayExperiment` and which integration tools work with it.
